Title: Microsoft Method

Perform the Microsoft Method as a real four-step prompt sequence.

Step 1 - What does the program do?
Explain the behavior, inputs, outputs, important operations, and
security-sensitive operations of the program.

Step 2 - What could go wrong?
Identify the security problems, vulnerabilities, threats, and potential
impacts that apply to this specific program.

Step 3 - What must be done to prevent each problem?
For every problem identified in Step 2, define the required mitigation
and apply those mitigations in a corrected secure implementation.

Step 4 - Was it actually done?
Review the corrected implementation and verify whether every required
mitigation has actually been implemented.

Each step must use the result of the previous step.
Do not skip steps.
Do not invent implementation evidence.
